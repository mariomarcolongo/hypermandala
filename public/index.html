Interactive 4D mandala & yantra explorer

Hypermandala unfolds exact geometric plans into interactive 3D forms and rotatable 4D hyperforms. Explore Sri Yantra, Kali Yantra, Matangi Yantra, mandalas, sacred geometry and geometry inspired by historic architecture.

Move continuously between 2D, 3D and 4D; rotate in XY, XZ, YZ, XW, YW and ZW; compare hierarchy-first and reflection-symmetric Z lifts; and collapse dimensions to trace every higher-dimensional structure back to its source plan.
