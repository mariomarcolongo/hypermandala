Hypermandala: interactive 4D mandala, yantra and geometry explorer

Hypermandala explores mandalas, yantras and geometric forms as dimensional structures: exact 2D geometric plans unfold into interactive 3D forms and experimental four-dimensional extensions.

Explore Sri Yantra, Kali Yantra, Matangi Yantra, square and hexagonal mandalas, plus architecture-inspired forms including Borobudur, stupa, Castel del Monte, Kukulcán and Bete Giyorgis. The 4D explorer supports rotations in all six coordinate planes: XY, XZ, YZ, XW, YW and ZW.

Higher-dimensional extensions are experimental mathematical visualizations. Where traditional 3D precedents exist, they inform the geometry without being presented as the only canonical interpretation.
